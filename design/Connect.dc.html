How tracking works
01 We pull every league that credential belongs to for the season — no typing league IDs one by one.
02 You tick the ones to track. Old and dead leagues stay out.
03 Each tracked league keeps its own scoring, roster rules, and redraft / dynasty setting. Nothing is shared across leagues.
Credentials are stored locally in data/ffdo.db, the same machine the app runs on.
